Arenza measures how 6 leading AI assistants — **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok** — describe a brand to its customers, across 4 markets (US, UK, DE, JP). Built **agency-first**: multi-brand portfolio, white-label reports, bulk CSV import, MCP server, public REST API.

## Two pillars

- **Visibility** — share of voice / mentions / citations across the 6 AI assistants, by brand / product line / SKU / locale.
- **Accuracy** — wrong-claim detection with severity scoring + verbatim quotes. Detect outdated specs, false claims, brand-attribution errors at the moment they appear in any AI answer.
