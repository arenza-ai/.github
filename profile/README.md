Arenza makes brands the recommendation **ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity** give buyers — then turns that recommendation into orders attributed in the customer's own store data. Run end-to-end as a managed service, paid on results (results are judged by the customer's store data, not by Arenza); a self-serve measurement tier remains for brands that only want tracking. Built for brand owners and the agencies that run their brands: multi-brand portfolio, white-label reports, bulk CSV import, MCP server, public REST API.

## The ARC growth model

**ARC = Answer · Reach · Convert.**

- **Answer** — be the answer AI gives. Measure mention rate, share of voice, sentiment, and per-engine rank across ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity in 4 markets (US, UK, DE, JP), and catch wrong claims — outdated specs, prices, integrations, brand-attribution errors — with severity scoring and the verbatim AI quote.
- **Reach** — cover more of the questions buyers actually ask. Publish AI-facing pages against the gap queries, idiomatic 12-language localization, and sponsored-slot tracking on AI shopping surfaces (Beta).
- **Convert** — turn that answer into an order. Structure product data so AI quotes it accurately, embed CTAs into AI-facing pages, attribute AI traffic and orders in the customer's own store data, and close checkout inside the AI surface (Beta).
